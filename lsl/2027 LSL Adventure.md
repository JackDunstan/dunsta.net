## Why Ireland is one visit, not two

Irish schools generally break up in the last week of June — after Glastonbury starts, not before. Putting Waterford first would land before Irish holidays begin. The fix: fly into London and head straight to Cornwall, then do the whole Ireland chapter — Waterford, then a night in Dublin — as one unbroken block immediately after Glastonbury, once school is properly out. Flying into London (rather than Dublin as originally drafted) removes the awkward same-day Dublin→Bristol connection and makes day one straightforward.

## Research findings behind the plan

- **Kent term dates (determined 2026–27):** last day of summer term Wed 21 July 2027; new year starts Wed 1 September 2027. Source: Kent County Council PDF.
- **Irish school holidays:** no single national fixed date; most primary schools close "around the last week of June 2027," reopen late Aug/early Sep. Source: HeadStart.ie.
- **Weather (driest month, May–Aug rainfall):** Dublin/Waterford best June–July; Cornwall best July (June close second); Edinburgh best June; Whitstable best July; **Islay best May**, worsening steadily through Jul–Aug. Late June — which Glastonbury already required — turned out to be close to optimal everywhere except Islay.
