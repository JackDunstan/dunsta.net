T. M. Dunleavy

The printed caption on Book 1, scan 010 names Rev. T. M. Dunleavy, assistant pastor at Queanbeyan. A handwritten note connects him with boxer Jack Dunleavy. That relationship is the compiler’s assertion and awaits independent confirmation.
