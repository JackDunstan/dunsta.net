Charles Hector McFadyen

Charles Hector McFadyen (1892–1965) served in the First World War and later led the Commonwealth Department of Shipping and Transport. The family identifies him as the compiler of these scrapbooks. Wikipedia cites two volumes held by the McFadyen family; that citation is corroborating context, not an independent authentication of every artifact.
