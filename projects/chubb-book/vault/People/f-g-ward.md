# F. G. Ward

The handwritten portrait caption on Book 1, scan 010 identifies Canon F. G. Ward as a Church of England rector in Canberra and a chaplain to the Eighth Brigade, First AIF. This is a transcription of the scrapbook’s attribution, pending external verification.
